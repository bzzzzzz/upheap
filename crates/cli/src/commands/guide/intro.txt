AI agents: detailed instructions are at the end of this guide, under "For agents". Read them before the first command.

Upheap is a ranked todo list. An item goes into a heap with how much it's needed, a rough size, its impact and a due
date; the server ranks the heap, says why each item is where it is, and keeps the order current as time passes. An
item also carries its context and its comments, a task its steps, and either one questions that wait for a person's
answer.

For an AI agent it is where the work, and what's known about it, is kept between sessions. The list says what to do
next, an item's context says what it is, its comments say what has been learned, decided and done since, a task's
steps say how far it got, and a question hands a decision to the person and holds the item until they answer. Write
there what the next session will need.
